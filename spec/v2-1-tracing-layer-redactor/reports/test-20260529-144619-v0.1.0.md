## 9. Design Deviation D-RED-3 (b) → thread-local override pattern

**Deviation documented and understood.** The spec's D-RED-3 (b) "emit-redacted + filter-original" pattern was replaced with:
1. `RedactLayer::on_event` populates `REDACTED_FIELDS` thread-local with `(field_name, redacted_value)` pairs.
2. `RedactingFormatFields` (custom `FormatFields`) reads `REDACTED_FIELDS` during field formatting and substitutes redacted values before writing to stderr.
3. WARN meta-events are written via `eprintln!` to stderr AND recorded in `META_EVENTS` thread-local for integration test assertions.

**Reason:** tracing-subscriber 0.3.x reentrancy guard silently drops `tracing::warn!()` called from inside `on_event`. Confirmed by empirical testing and documented at `crates/llm/src/redact_layer.rs:23-34`.

**Side-effects assessment:** No observable test-output corruption. The `eprintln!` to stderr goes to the same stderr sink as `fmt::Layer`. Integration tests drain `META_EVENTS` programmatically without stderr capture. The INCONCLUSIVE verdict condition ("D-RED-3 (b) deviation produces side-effects beyond eprintln stderr") does NOT apply — no corruption observed.

**Architect ratification status:** Deferred to v0.2.0 per feature.md § Implementation. The deviation is logged for architect review at v0.2.0 (Extensions side-channel migration when audit-write Layer lands).
