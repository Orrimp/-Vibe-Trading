panel: kill
title: Stop trading
state: idle
button: Stop trading
help: Cancels open orders, flattens every position, and halts the agent. Requires a typed confirmation.

notes:
  - The kill-switch panel has no intrinsic "loading" state — the agent
    bus is event-driven, not query-driven, so there is no waiting period.
    This artifact documents the pre-interaction idle view which functions
    as the panel's "loading equivalent" — a quiet neutral state before
    any operator action has occurred.
