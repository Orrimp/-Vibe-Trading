## 6. Open Observation for Tester Pickup

**Marker count discrepancy.** The cockpit fixtures spawn 4 fills per
symbol (per `synthetic_fills_for(_, _, 4)` at `crates/ui/src/bin/cockpit.rs:165`).
In `/tmp/cockpit-T2032-1920x1080.png` the orchestrator counted **2
clearly visible triangles** on the price line — one upward green Buy
around $39,950, one downward red Sell around $39,800. The expected
4 should sit at minutes 12 / 24 / 36 / 48 of the 60-minute fixture
range (per the precursor commit `a8e7110` that spread fills across
the bar range).

Three plausible explanations, **none investigated** in M6.2-hardening:

1. **Markers stacking on line vertices** — the price line happens to
   pass through the marker centroid at the same pixel; the triangle
   blends into the line at this capture resolution.
2. **Triangle size too small at the capture scale** — 13-px triangle
   becomes ~3px at the screenshot's downscale; difficult to count.
3. **Positioning bug** — some markers fall outside `inner_rect` and
   get clipped by the `anchor_for_ts` early-return at
   `crates/ui/src/widgets/chart.rs:363-365`. Unlikely given a8e7110
   spread fills explicitly within the bar range, but possible if
   the bar range now differs from the fixture timestamps.

The tester at `T_FINAL_CHART_BUY_SELL_EMPHASIS` should manually
verify the full 4-marker expectation in a live cockpit window
before VERDICT → PASS. If markers are still 2-of-4 visible, that's
a real defect that routes back to ui-designer.
