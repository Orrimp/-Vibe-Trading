ADR-0078 · commit 9433e35 P1-4 · HONEST REPOSITION

The vol overlay is a risk tool, not a Sharpe tool

Reframed the existing volatility overlay to promise smoother drawdowns, never a higher Sharpe — because on crypto it mechanistically can't deliver one.

On equities, cutting size when volatility spikes raises risk-adjusted return (the leverage effect). On crypto that effect is reversed (γ ≈ −0.261 — volatility spikes after rallies, FOMO-driven), so the Sharpe mechanism simply isn't there. The overlay now uses a slow EWMA + a no-trade band (bounds churn) + de-risk-only (never levers up), and reports a per-coin correlation telling you whether the Sharpe mechanism is even present. Default behaviour is unchanged — the honest posture is an explicit opt-in.

ADR-0080 · commit 9433e35 P1-3 · THE €200 FLOOR

Drawdown guard — "never lose more than 20%"

A new overlay that shrinks exposure as losses approach a floor, promising: never lose more than 20% of the starting €200.

Static floor (CPPI @ 20%, your ratified D8 choice) plus a load-bearing high-water-mark restart — the benchmark is stark: on BTC with 0.1% costs, with the restart it held Sharpe 1.52 and cut max-drawdown 72%→20%; the same controller without the restart collapsed to Sharpe −0.04. It composes with the budget cap (never bypasses it).

6-test day-1 divergence e2e (proves it's not a no-op)HWM-restart + floor-never-moves + budget-cap proofsanchors 119/119
An honest catch worth noting: the drawdown formula cited in the research (M = (d_max−d)/(1−d)) evaluates to "only 20% exposure when you're winning" — the wrong direction. The dev caught it and used the corrected normalised form (M(0)=1.0 at a new high, M=0 at the floor), documented in ADR-0080 §D2. The research prose ("scale exposure up with the cushion") confirms the correction is what was intended.

test-only · commit a43bf3f P2-2 · THE CAPSTONE

Proof the gate can't be fooled by noise

A test that runs the full bake-off on pure noise — and proves the credibility gate rejects it.

It feeds the bake-off random walks (GBM), volatility-clustering (GARCH), and mean-reversion (OU) — data with no real edge — across 15 seeded runs, and asserts no lucky "winner" survives as credible. The striking result:

The primary gate alone crowned an active strategy on pure noise ~1 in 5 times (a random walk "beaten" by RSI!). The honesty scorecard caught every single one (deflated-confidence ~0.4–0.8, all below the 0.95 bar). This is the empirical proof that the scorecard you shipped in Phase 2A is load-bearing, not decorative — it catches exactly what the primary gate misses. You reviewed this and kept the scorecard report-only (it shows the crown and its low confidence, side by side); the veto switch stays ready if you ever want it.
