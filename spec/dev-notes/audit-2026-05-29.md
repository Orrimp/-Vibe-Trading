REVISION.toml aggregate change from a FETCH operation (not a wiring bug). Analyst
should confirm whether the v0.1.3 in-place SHA update under the v0.1.1 namespace
correctly invokes the D6.b re-emission exception, or whether a new namespace was
required. Confidence: medium. Owner: analyst (ADR-0038 exception scope
clarification).
