## Meta-patterns observed

### 1. Durable contract → spontaneous over-delivery on test surface

The durable-over-quick contract landed 2026-05-28; Wave A of
`v3-regime-classifier` shipped **14 unit tests against ~3 planned**
(developer agent `a2b1a58356c71dd01` shipped Wave A in ~30 minutes vs
3–6 h estimate, with **zero MIGRATION: comments**, citing the trait-frozen-
at-v0.1.0 + Hamilton-1989 priors as the natural test surface). The
`v5-latency-slippage-sim-v0.4.0` ship the same day followed the same
pattern. The contract didn't just shift *quality* — it shifted *effort
direction*: developers spent the freed wall-clock on additional safety
nets rather than next-feature pickup.

### 2. v3 retirement as VINDICATION of the durable contract

`v3-regime-classifier` shipped 5 dev waves over 2 days (A: Markov-
switching core; B: K4 RegimeTag extension; C: dispatcher with cash-
fallback; D: audit RegimeTag + Trail UI column; E: 4 anchored backtest
reports). Wave E surfaced **net Sharpe-delta −0.294113** on the 2024
held-out validation against un-overlaid v1 momentum; classifier failed
H2 (V-REG-5 "fails to separate regimes meaningfully"). Operator picked
**R-O: RETIRE**.

The vindication: **zero MIGRATION: debt across all 5 waves; zero anchor
re-emission cost; zero cleanup-brief debt; zero v0.2.0 follow-on debt**
— the gates worked **exactly as designed**, identifying an empty
strategy without accumulating sunk cost in the codebase. Anchored Wave E
bodies (75/75 total) stay as scientific record per ADR-0038 § D6.a.
**This is the strongest empirical evidence that the durable contract
works end-to-end** — a feature that fails its alpha gate doesn't
become a 6-month cleanup tail.

### 3. Operator-override rate on analyst `(Recommended)` tags — pre-durable era

At `v3-regime-classifier` M-OD (commit `6b47027`, 2026-05-28 PRE-durable-
codification), the operator overrode **3 of 5** analyst defaults
(Q1 4-state over 3-state; Q3 Markov-switching over HMM; Q4 strategy-
switching dispatcher over overlay), keeping defaults on Q2 + Q5. Cost

This pattern is **exactly what the durable contract codifies** — and the
2026-05-29 strengthening (`always suggest parallel options`) is the
orchestrator-side echo: operator's revealed preference is *more bold
options surfaced earlier*, not *fewer cheap options surfaced safely*.

### 4. Mid-flight orphan-folder spec-lint cross-talk

When analyst spawns in parallel with a tester or presenter mid-write,
the folder briefly exists with `feature.md` only (no `tasks.md` yet),
and any in-flight spec-lint sweep flags it as missing-tasks. Concrete
case: the `v0.4.0` candle re-emit tester `test-final-2026-05-28` report
records **`spec-lint: FAIL (77 violations in 4 categories)`** including
4 `trace-broken-path` rows owned by the parallel `v3-regime-classifier`
Wave E mid-write. Tester correctly carved out as not-v0.4.0-attributable
and PASS held — but the pattern is real and recurring.

**Mitigation**: parallel-spawn briefs should declare expected spec-lint
deltas in the carve-out section so tester / presenter can pre-allocate
attribution.
