### SC-1 — `feature.md` status enum vs `trace.toml` state enum (HIGH confidence)

**Topic:** Workflow-state vocabulary alignment.
- `spec/cross-sectional-mean-reversion-strategy/feature.md` has `status:
  tested`. This is **NOT** in `scripts/spec_lint.py` VALID_STATUSES (the
  recognised tokens are `arch-done | dev-done | tester-done |
  presenter-done`). The lint correctly flags it as `missing-frontmatter`.
- `spec/trace.toml` row `REQ-XS-MEANREVERSION-001` has `state =
  "tested"`. This **is** an accepted trace state token per the header
  comment at `spec/trace.toml:17` (`draft | proposed | candidate |
  in-progress | roadmap | shipped | deprecated` plus historical
  `tested`).
- The two enums disagree on a single token: `tested` exists in trace
  vocabulary but not in feature.md vocabulary. The intent at
  `feature.md` is clearly `tester-done` (post-tester-PASS,
  pre-presenter). A trivial-change frontmatter flip closes this.

**Confidence: HIGH.** The intent and fix are unambiguous. This is the
same drift class flagged in `spec-audit-2026-05-31.md` SHOULD-FIX-1 —
that audit said the value should flip from `dev-done` to `tester-done`,
but a subsequent edit chose `tested` (non-enum) instead of `tester-done`
(enum). The lesson from the C1 (`monte-carlo-bootstrap-path-generator`)
recurrence two days ago to MR today: the tester PASS checklist should
explicitly require `status: tester-done` (the exact enum token), not
just "advance the status". Owner: orchestrator.
