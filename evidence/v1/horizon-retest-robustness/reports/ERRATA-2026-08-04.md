# ERRATA — anchors #92–#99 (horizon-retest-robustness θ-surfaces)

**Issued 2026-08-04 by the story-1-18 code review. The anchored report bodies
in this directory are byte-frozen and unmodified (ADR-0038); this additive
companion is the correction of record.**

## 1. The carry × coarse-horizon surfaces (#96–#99) measured a THROTTLED mechanism

Funding settlement is detected as `hours_since_epoch % 8 == 0`, computed from
the generated bar's timestamp — but `BlockBootstrapPathGen` re-stamps every
generated path on a **cosmetic 1-hour ladder** (`Timeframe::OneHour` hardcoded,
`epoch_base + Duration::hours(i)`) regardless of the source horizon. The gate
therefore counts **bars, not hours**:

| Lane | Settlement fires every | Should be | Per-path funding harvested (g=0, 2023) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1h (#88, reference) | 8 bars = 8 h ✅ | 8 h | **15,490** |
| 4h (#96/#97) | 8 bars = **32 h** | 8 h | **3,039** |
| daily (#98/#99) | 8 bars = **8 days** | 3× per bar | **267** |

A carry strategy exists to harvest funding. On the coarse lanes it harvested a
fraction of it, so **#96–#99 did not measure carry-at-a-coarse-horizon; they
measured carry with most of its cashflow removed.**

**Consequently the following claim, which appears in the hashed bodies of
#96–#99, is NOT supported by those runs:**

> "Even at the native settlement cadence, funding mean-reversion or directional
> price exposure overwhelmed the funding harvest."

The native settlement cadence was never simulated on those lanes.

**Status of the conclusion:** the carry × {4h, daily} leg of the
"OHLCV thesis closed across method AND horizon" claim is **UNRESOLVED pending
the story-1-25 re-lock**, not merely imprecise. It is neither confirmed nor
refuted by the present evidence. The TS × {4h, daily} legs (#92–#95) are
unaffected by this defect and stand *direction-preserved pending re-lock* on
the same footing as #86/#87/#90/#91 (bug-log #67).

## 2. Scope of the headline claim

"closed across method and horizon" as a programme-level conclusion resting on
measurement **plus** inference — and, per §1, with the carry-coarse quarter of

## 4b. CORRECTION to §1 (issued the same day, 2026-08-04)

§1 above quoted per-path harvest figures 15,490 → 3,039 → 267 and drew the
ratios "~1/4" and "~1/24" from them. **Those ratios are withdrawn.** A second,
larger defect was found while building the fix (bug-log **#73**): funding was
accrued once per SYMBOL-BAR rather than once per settlement, because the accrual
block sat inside a loop over multi-symbol merged bars with no per-timestamp
dedup. Measured directly — one held position, only the universe size varying —
the total came out −5 / −7 / −9 units for 2 / 3 / 4 symbols. The anchored
surfaces use a 10-symbol universe.

Consequences for this errata:

- The 1h carry surfaces (#88/#89), used above as the "reference", are **not** a
  reference: they over-accrue by roughly the universe size.
- The direction of §1 stands — coarse-horizon carry under-settles relative to
  its own cadence — but its **magnitude** cannot be computed from the old
  numbers, because both sides of that ratio were contaminated.
- The corrected magnitudes come from the story-1-25 re-run under the fixed
  accrual, not from arithmetic on the frozen bodies.

Both defects are now FIXED IN CODE (dedup + explicit `bar_span_hours`, with two
measurement-derived regression gates in `carry_divergence_e2e.rs`). The anchored
bodies remain frozen and unreproducible by the current code — which is precisely
what the 1-25 re-lock exists to resolve. **The carry leg of the thesis closure
remains UNRESOLVED**, and now for two independent reasons rather than one.
