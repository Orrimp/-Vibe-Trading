## Noted divergences (not regressions)

Per `feature.md ## Implementation ## Architectural divergences from architect's design`, the developer surfaced 4 intentional design refinements. These are operator-acceptable and do not drop the verdict:

1. **M2-A scope** — `tracing::trace_span!` lands on widget **constructor functions** (`panel`, `loading_with_spinner`, `id_cell`) rather than `Widget::draw` / `Widget::layout` impls. The `ui` crate's widgets are mostly functional builders returning `Element`, not custom `Widget` impls; constructor-fn level is where the F1-class regression signature surfaces during view-tree build. Documented inline at `widgets/frame.rs:54-64,182-192` and `widgets/strategies.rs:218-235`.
2. **M2-B newtype generic vs concrete** — `DebugRenderer<R: Renderer>` is generic over the inner renderer rather than wrapping concrete `iced_tiny_skia::Renderer` directly, because `iced_tiny_skia` is not a direct dep of the `ui` crate (transitively pulled via iced's `tiny-skia` feature). Net behaviour preserved.
3. **M2-B runtime wiring deferred** — iced 0.14's `Application` builder API does not accept a custom renderer; `DebugRenderer` is diagnostic-only (panic + unit tests prove behaviour) until either upstream change or intrusive patch lands. Surfaced in `tasks.md` T-M2-B-2 `[open_questions]`.
4. **M1-C invariant relaxed to root-Node-only** — full-tree walk produced false positives on stock widget patterns (`Space::new()`, padding-only Containers); root-Node check still catches the F1-class top-level-Container-collapses signature. Documented inline at `layout_invariants.rs:67-102`.

All four are intentional and traceable.
