**Scope of that verdict, stated precisely** (per the 2026-06-06 adjudication): this is a **vehicle** verdict, not a signal verdict. A long-only tilt carries full market beta and captures only one leg of what is inherently a spread, so benchmarking it against buy-and-hold sets a bar the vehicle cannot clear regardless of signal quality — which is exactly why story 1-21 was built to strip the beta. 1-21's own reported outcome (*"MN 3-arm sweep — all FRAGILE, basis carries no orthogonal alpha"*, commit `963b0fe`) closes the signal question too, but that result is itself **pending its own review** — next in the burn-down, with anchors #108-#119 carrying the same inherited chain. Neither verdict should be quoted as final until 1-21 is reviewed and 1-25 re-locks.
