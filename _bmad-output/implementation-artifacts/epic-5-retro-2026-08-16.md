## What this epic actually was

The attempt to *prove the project finished* rather than add to it: re-run the verdict on new data,
make the crown honest about its own credibility, enforce point-in-time discipline, add €200 realism,
and hand the plan off to the real world.

## What went well

- **5-2 is the strongest thing in this epic**: re-running the frozen gate on 4 new corpora including a
  second venue is genuine falsification effort — the project tried to break its own headline claim on
  new data and reported that it held. Most projects never do this.
- **5-4 produced the best-engineered gate in the repository.** Independently re-verified 2026-08-15:
  private `AsOf` fields, a single constructor, a **trybuild compile-fail test**, and a shape-matching
  lint with a self-test that plants a violation. It cannot be satisfied by accident.
- **Every story landed with a numbered ADR** (0083, 0085–0088). The decision trail for this epic is
  intact, which is exactly what made it auditable a month later.
- **5-6 golden-locked operator-ratified wording** rather than paraphrasing it — the serialiser cannot
  drift from what the operator approved.

## What did not go well

- **5-3 co-presents a gate that fails open.** Bug-log **#83** (found 2026-08-15): a robustness run
  that *could not be computed* returns `Skipped`, and `rank.rs::is_eligible` treats everything except
  `Fragile` as crown-eligible. The credibility surface this story built sits on top of a gate whose
  failure mode is "pass". Not 5-3's fault — but it is 5-3's exposure.
- **The credibility verdict is not witnessed in the evidence corpus.** None of the 62 anchored reports
  render the robustness flag at all (`grep -i robust|fragile|marginal` → 0 hits), so the corpus cannot
  show whether the gate has ever fired. An honesty surface whose decisions leave no trace in the
  frozen record is weaker than it looks.
- **5-5's realism is opt-in and off by default**, so the shipped default is still the less realistic
  path. Defensible (anchor stability) but worth stating.

## Insight

**This epic proved the *result* and under-proved the *machinery*.** 5-2 falsification-tested the
headline claim on new data — excellent. But the gate producing that claim had a fail-open path
(#83) and leaves no trace in the anchored record. The verdict was stress-tested; the verdict's
instrument was not.
