## What this epic actually was

The tranche that built the project's honesty machinery — the layer that decides how confident the
advisor is allowed to sound.

Shipped: overfitting scorecard (P0-1, report-only, surfaced #1 in 6 of 9 research topics) ·
turnover + tail metrics (Churn column, CVaR risk-story block) · **confidence-not-verdict** reframing
(P0-3) · forward-fidelity coverage R1 (all 14 post-F5b arms forward-covered) · shared σ̂ vol
estimator (P1-5) · vol-overlay reposition as de-risk-only, never a crown input (P1-4) ·
drawdown-control overlay with CPPI-style floor (P1-3) · opt-in cost model (P1-6) · narration
faithfulness with verbatim-number matching + banned-phrase list (P2-1) · no-alpha gate CI ·
data-quality surface.

## What went well

- **"Confidence, not verdict" is the single best framing decision in the product.** It converts an
  overclaiming surface into an honest one, and it is upstream of nearly everything the advisor says.
- **R1 closed a real coverage hole rather than papering over it** — all 14 post-F5b arms became
  forward-covered, retiring the known 14-arm residual.
- **The overlays were positioned honestly**: vol-targeting is *de-risk-only and never a crown input*.
  That constraint is what stops a sizing modifier from quietly becoming an alpha claim.
- **Narration faithfulness was hardened with verbatim-number matching**, not vibes — the LLM cannot
  restate a number the data does not contain.

## What did not go well

- **A decision this epic made was never written down.** Bug-log **#86**: story 4-5's ADR-**0079** had
  a Registry row, was cited by shipped source (`vol_estimator.rs:1`, `strategy/lib.rs:49`) and by
  **ADR-0078's `Consumes:` line** — but **the file never existed**. The corpus ran 86 files against 87
  rows for ~6 weeks, and `adr_registry_check.py` could not see it because the lint was one-directional.
  Fixed 2026-08-15 (file reconstructed from primary sources + converse check `(d)` added, RED-proven).
- **The "one shared estimator" goal is half-delivered.** 4-5 built the estimator for two consumers.
  4-7's `drawdown_control_overlay` **never references it**, and **3 of the 4 functions** — including
  the full Corsi-2009 HAR-RV — have **zero production callers**. The consistency the epic was buying
  is currently bought for one consumer.
- **The `#[allow(dead_code)]` habit shows up here.** Related finding #89: a seeded RNG constructed and
  never read, with the compiler's own warning silenced.

## Insight

**Epic 4 built the honesty machinery and did not apply that machinery to itself.** It shipped the
scorecard, the confidence reframing, the faithfulness checker — and simultaneously shipped a cited
ADR that did not exist, a shared estimator with one of two consumers wired, and three exported
functions nobody calls. The epic was rigorous about what the *product* claims and comparatively
relaxed about what the *repo* claims.

That is the same declared-vs-executed shape found everywhere in the 2026-08 sweep, and Epic 4 is
where it is most ironic.
