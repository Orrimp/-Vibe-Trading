## What this epic actually was

A sustained bet that a learned model could beat the passive baseline: a 4-phase DL programme
(TCN → PatchTST → vanilla Transformer → v2.6 bake-off), an LLM forecaster, a GARCH-σ volatility
forecaster, and two OHLCV regime classifiers.

## What went well

- **It reached honest negative verdicts and acted on them.** "MODEL-BROKEN / NO-ALPHA … RETIRED" is
  a hard thing to write about your own work. The epic retired arms instead of keeping them alive on
  hope, and the do-not-build register exists so they are not re-proposed.
- **The failure was converted into a durable invariant.** The `v3-volatility-forecaster-noop-fix`
  discovery — an overlay that computed `scale` and never applied it — became **AD-16**: every
  overlay or sizing modifier ships with a baseline-equity-divergence e2e from day 1. That is the
  single most valuable artifact this epic produced, and it is not a model.
- **Foreclosure was principled.** The classifiers were stopped because the *channel* was exhausted,
  not because a run disappointed — a reusable stopping rule rather than a mood.

## What did not go well

- **Highest spend, lowest yield.** ~153 commits across ten slugs for zero shipped alpha. That is a
  legitimate research outcome, but it is the epic's defining economic fact and should be said plainly.
- **An entire arm was measured while inert.** The no-op overlay means results were being read off a
  modifier that never modified anything. Every conclusion drawn before 2026-05-22 was drawn from a
  disconnected instrument.
- **The LLM arm never got its verdict.** 7-6 is `done` but "shipped-partial" pending an API key, so
  the board reads complete where the science does not.

## The insight worth carrying — and it stings

**The invariant this epic paid for was later implemented in ways that could not fail.**

AD-16 exists *because* of 7-11. Yet the 2026-08 review burn-down found the mandated AD-16 gate was
**vacuous in multiple later stories** — bug-log **#74** (story 1-20: the gate injected its signal
through a channel production does not use) and story 3-15 (three vacuous gates, a 10 %-invested arm
compared against a 100 % benchmark). The rule was written down and obeyed *in form*, and nobody
checked that the obeying artifact could actually fail.

So the lesson from Epic 7 is not "write down what you learned." It is: **a lesson recorded as a rule
is not enforced until something proves the enforcement can fail.** The epic paid full price for the
insight and the insight then decayed into ritual.
