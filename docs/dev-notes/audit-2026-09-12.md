### F-D — CARRY-OVER, UNACTIONED and now DOUBLY EVIDENCED (P1). AD-2 has been falsified in two orthogonal ways; the clause still reads as originally written

Baseline established that `verify_anchors.sh` measures **immutability**, not **reproducibility**
(#93), and that the four re-running tests that *do* measure reproducibility are `#[ignore]`d.
Re-verified at HEAD: **all four `#[ignore]` attributes are still present, verbatim**, with the
correct do-not-re-baseline reasoning inline. `#93`'s `Status:` still reads OPEN.

**#97 adds a second, independent failure mode of the same clause.** AD-2 says the 119 bodies are
byte-identical before and after any change. Until `d46c388` (2026-08-29) that was **also**
conditional on the checkout's line-ending policy — a Windows checkout produced 81 CRLFs and a
different SHA for every anchored report, and the two failing tests
(`t1937_nine_strategy_anchors_unchanged`, `t1937b_canonical_strategy_anchors_unchanged`) were
exactly that, observed on the `windows-latest` leg. The `.gitattributes` header states the
principle better than the constitution does:

> A byte-immutability invariant that depends on which OS checked the repo out is not an invariant.

So the ledger now reads:

| AD-2's guarantee, as written        | Actual status at HEAD                                                        |
|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
