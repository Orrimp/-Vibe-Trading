Feature 1 of 3 · ADR-0075 reserved

P0-1The Honesty Scorecard

TL;DR

Every bake-off recommendation now carries an honesty check next to it that answers the one question the whole product rests on — "did we fool ourselves by trying many strategies?" In plain language: strategies tried · deflated confidence · minimum history needed · beats-holding-after-the-search?

It is report-only — never changes the pick above. The frozen robustness gate is proven byte-identical before and after the scorecard runs. Most of the time it will confirm "just hold." That's the point.

What the operator sees — the modal "holding wins" case

[ Recommendation ]
No active strategy cleared the robustness bar on BTCUSDT —
simply holding (buy-and-hold) is the least-bad choice on this window.

[ Ranked table ]
#  Strategy        Return    Sharpe   Max-DD     Trades
1  v0.buyhold ★    +11.24%   0.6900   −13.38%        2
2  v0.sma           +1.43%   0.2100   −15.21%       41

[ How much to trust this ]      ← NEW
An honesty check on the search behind the pick — it never changes
the result.

  STRATEGIES TRIED
  2 — about 2 truly independent

  DEFLATED CONFIDENCE
  38%
  Chance the edge is real after accounting for how many we tried.

  MINIMUM HISTORY NEEDED
  about 1.1 years of data
  Trust the result only with at least this much history behind it.

  BEATS HOLDING AFTER THE SEARCH?
  ✗ Not clearly — holding is the honest call
  Informational, not a gate — this never changes the pick above.

Read those rows together. The verdict above (buy-and-hold crowned) and the four facts below describe the same picture: only two strategies tried, the deflated confidence is modest, and the search hasn't beaten holding. The scorecard isn't telling you "you missed an edge" — it's confirming the search was honest and the modest verdict is the right one.

Feature 2 of 3 · ADR-0076 reserved

P1Cost story + Risk story

TL;DR

The leaderboard used to stop at Return/Sharpe/MaxDD/Trades + a headline. v2 fills the two missing chapters: the cost story (a Churn column on every row — turnover as a multiple of capital) and the risk story (a six-fact panel: typical outcome, CVaR-95, CVaR-99, skew, Sortino, Calmar).

Both are report-only. The frozen gate is byte-identical before and after (proven by turnover_does_not_change_ranking). CVaR — not VaR: CVaR is coherent (the risk of a combined portfolio never exceeds the sum of parts'); plain VaR isn't, and rewards concentration over diversification.

What the operator sees — the same modal "holding wins" case

[ How much to trust this ]       (P0-1 scorecard — existing)

[ Risk story ]                  ← NEW
  Typical outcome (median)                    102,300 USDT

  Average loss in the worst 5% of paths         −18.0%
  Average loss in the worst 1% of paths         −31.0%

Read the rows together. The headline crowns buy-and-hold. The Churn column confirms why: holding transacts nothing (0.0×) while the SMA churns ~5× its capital — the active strategy is paying for its modest +1.43% return in fees. The Risk story explains the honest range: middle path lands at €102,300, worst-5% paths lose 18%, worst-1% lose 31%; the shape is mildly right-skewed. That's the whole story — why holding wins, not just that it wins.

P0-3Confidence check, not verdict

TL;DR

The forward paper-trade screen used to read as a fresh verdict. Now it reads as a confidence check on the crown the bake-off already chose. Same paper trade, same €200, same standing rules — the relabel just says out loud what the screen has always been.

A 4-row "How much to trust this pick" block now appears below the plan — the same scorecard the operator read on the leaderboard while crowning. So "watching it" never gets confused with "a new bet being placed." One consistent honesty surface across ANALYSIS and SUGGESTION.

What the operator sees — fixture: "the search ran wide, edge is uncertain"

[ Confidence check ]           ← new headline (was "Your plan")
Watching the crowned strategy as new bars arrive — a confidence
check on that pick, not a fresh prediction or a guarantee of
future edge. The same rules your simulated €200 paper-trade runs.

…  (existing plan blocks: framing banner, stance, rules, sizing,
                                                            horizon …)

[ How much to trust this pick ]   ← NEW 4-row block
This is a confidence check on the crowned pick, not a fresh verdict.
The pick was chosen in the bake-off; this is how much to trust that choice.

  STRATEGIES TRIED
  18
  Each extra strategy tried raises the bar for the winner.

  DEFLATED CONFIDENCE
  87%
  Probability the pick's edge is real after correcting for the number
  of tries. Above 95% is the honest bar.

  BEATS HOLDING?
  ⚠ Not yet — edge uncertain after the search
  Informational only — never a rule change. The pick stands regardless.

  MINIMUM HISTORY NEEDED
  6.4 yr
  Years of data required to distinguish real edge from luck at this
  trial count.

The confidence block is informational — it does not change the pick
or the rules.

Read the relabel + the four rows together. The search tried 18 strategies; the crown's deflated confidence sits at 87% (under the 95% honest bar); it has not yet shown it beats holding after the search; you'd want 6.4 years of data to call this real. The plan above still runs the same rules and the same €200 — the operator just sees the credibility of that pick out loud while it paper-trades.
