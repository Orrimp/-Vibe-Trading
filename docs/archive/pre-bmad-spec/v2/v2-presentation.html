Trading Framework · Version 2

The Honest Advisor

A framework for traceable and plausible trading — it shows its work, so you can trust the answer.
You pick one coin and a small budget. The framework bakes off every strategy, ranks them honestly, and tells you what to do with your money — and how much to trust it. v2 makes the whole journey end-to-end and finally makes the “why” visible.
The idea in one minute

We sell honesty, not promises

Nine independent reviews of 900 research papers reached the same conclusion: on a single coin, no active strategy reliably beats simply holding, once you pay the trading costs. That sounds like bad news — it’s actually our superpower.

Most tools shout “buy this, it’ll go up!” Ours does the opposite: it runs every strategy against your coin, and tells you the truth — usually “just hold,” sometimes “this rule looks better, and here’s exactly how confident we are.” The value isn’t a magic money-maker. The value is a tool you can believe.

v2 is about making that honesty complete and visible: a single end-to-end flow from raw data to a clear buy/sell suggestion, with the confidence and the risk shown right next to it.

Why v2

What’s missing today — and what you’ll see instead

Today’s screen

It shows the basics and picks a winner…

  • Return, Sharpe, Max-Drawdown, Trade count
  • A single highlighted recommendation

…but it stays silent on the things that decide whether to believe it:

  • How many strategies we tried (is the “winner” just luck?)
  • The deflated confidence after that search
  • The trading costs / turnover behind the number
  • The tail & drawdown risk — how bad the bad days get
  • A clear start-to-finish journey

v2’s screen

Everything above, plus the “show your work” layer:

  • Honesty scorecard next to every pick — confidence after accounting for how many strategies we tried
  • “Beats holding?” stated plainly, never assumed
  • Cost story — the turnover that explains the net result
  • Risk story — drawdown & tail, not just average return
  • The Data → Training → Analysis → Suggestion spine tying it together
What we’ll build

The v2 features

Small, additive, honest. Each one makes the advice more trustworthy — none of them is a bet on “more profit.”

P0 · BUILD FIRST

The Honesty Scorecard

“Did we fool ourselves by trying many strategies?” — answered, every time.

What you’ll see
For the crowned strategy: how many we tried, a deflated confidence, a minimum-history check, and a clear “does this really beat holding?” verdict.
Why it matters
This is the literal meaning of “traceable & plausible.” It’s the credibility layer the whole product rests on — and it was the #1 finding across the research.
Honest framing
Most of the time it will confirm “just hold.” That’s the point — it stops a lucky fluke from being sold as a winner.
R1 · GROUNDWORK

Faithful “watch your money”

When you watch your €200 trade forward, it runs the exact strategy that won — not a stand-in.

What you’ll see
The forward paper-trade plays the real crowned strategy for every strategy type the bake-off can crown.
Why it matters
“Watch the selected strategy” has to be literally true. This closes the gap and must land before anything new is added.
P1 · NEXT

Smart risk control

The one place active management genuinely helps — a smoother ride, not bigger returns.

What you’ll see
A drawdown guard (caps how deep losses go) and volatility-based sizing (smaller bets when the market is wild), with the trade-off shown honestly.
Why it matters
On crypto you can’t reliably buy more return — but you can cut the worst drawdowns. We’ll sell it as exactly that: risk reduction, with the cost (some upside given up) stated plainly.

The outcome

What we’ll achieve

An advisor you can trust because it shows its work — the honest answer (often “hold”), with the confidence and the risk visible right beside it.

  • A complete, guided journey from real data to a concrete buy/sell/how-much suggestion.
  • Every recommendation is auditable — you can see how many strategies were tried and how confident the verdict really is.
  • Honest risk — drawdown and tail shown, with an optional guard to smooth the ride.

  • Reproducible — same inputs, same answer; nothing hidden.
Deliberate boundaries

What we will not build (and why)

Staying honest means saying no to the tempting-but-fake. These would pull the framework off-mission.

✕
Picking the “best of many coins.”
You choose one coin. Spreading across many coins doesn’t protect you in a crypto crash (everything falls together), and the edges that exist there are a different product entirely.
✕
AI that predicts the price.
The research is unanimous: price/return prediction doesn’t beat a coin-flip once you pay costs. We won’t pretend otherwise. (AI helps explain, and we can forecast volatility for sizing — never the price.)
✕
Auto-hunting for a winning strategy.
Letting a machine search thousands of strategies is the fastest way to fool yourself — it finds flukes. Our honesty gate exists precisely to prevent this.
✕
An AI that places trades.
The AI narrates and explains the real numbers; it never decides what to buy. That bright line stays.

The thread through all of it

Three principles

Measured, not asserted. Every claim is earned against a “just hold” benchmark and a resampling stress-test — never assumed.
Risk-shaping, not alpha. Where active management helps at all, it’s by cutting drawdowns — we say that plainly and never promise more profit.
Show the work. Confidence, costs, and risk are surfaced next to every suggestion. If we can’t prove it, we don’t crown it.
